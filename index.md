# Privacy Guard – Privacy policy

_Last updated 21 October 2026_

Developer contact: **ahsan.malik15@hotmail.com**

## Summary
Privacy Guard works entirely on your phone. It has no internet permission, contains no ads, analytics or tracking, and never sends any information about you or your phone to us or anyone else.

## What the app reads on your phone
- **Installed apps:** the list of installed apps and the permissions and special access (accessibility, notification access, device admin) each one has, to show which apps can reach your personal data.
- **Network usage (only if you grant Usage access):** how many bytes each app sent over the network, to spot background uploads. Only amounts are read, never the content.
- **Phone security settings:** screen lock, USB debugging, developer options, installed security certificates, signs of root, device management apps and the security patch date.

## What is stored, and where
Scan results, your blocked and trusted app lists, settings and up to 8 weekly reports are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. They are excluded from cloud backup and phone-to-phone transfer.

## The firewall (VPN)
If you block apps, Privacy Guard sets up a VPN on your phone. Only the apps you block are routed into it and their traffic is discarded on the phone. Traffic is not inspected, logged or stored, and no traffic from any app is sent to a server. Other apps' traffic does not go through the VPN.

## Sharing
Privacy Guard does not collect, sell or share any personal data. Nothing leaves your phone.

## Notifications
Alerts and the weekly report are created on your phone. You can turn them off in Settings.

## App updates
Updates are offered through Google Play's in-app update feature, which is run by the Google Play Store app under Google's own privacy policy. Privacy Guard does not send data for this.

## Keeping and deleting data
Data stays until you delete it. Uninstalling Privacy Guard, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, permanently deletes everything it stored.

## Children
Privacy Guard is not directed at children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page and in the app.
